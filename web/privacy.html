StrokeSmriti privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026.

StrokeSmriti is a free tool that clinicians use to record a patient's cognitive assessment after a stroke or a stroke procedure. It is a research tool. It is not validated and does not give a diagnosis.

What the app collects

Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server. It works without an internet connection.

What stays on the device

While an assessment is in progress, the answers are kept on the device so that the session survives an accidental close. The app asks for a study ID and does not ask for the patient's name. The saved session is deleted once the examiner copies the results or the visit code. In the Android app, this data is excluded from device backups.

What the examiner can copy

At the end of an assessment the examiner can copy the results as text or as a visit code, or show the visit code as a QR image. These leave the device only if the examiner pastes, sends or prints them. Handling that copy is the responsibility of the examiner and their institution, under their own consent and ethics procedures.

Permissions

The Android app requests internet access only because the app framework requires it. The app loads no online content.

Children

The app is meant for use by clinicians with adult patients.

Changes

If a future version offers to upload data, this policy will change before that version is released, and uploading will need ethics approval and patient consent at each site.
