# Lines awaiting translation

These patient-facing lines have no translation yet in Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Malayalam, Odia, Bengali or Telugu. The app shows the English text with "translation pending" until they arrive. When a language's lines are added, change that language's entry in `TVER` in `web/index.html`.

| Key | English |
|---|---|
| `bisect` | Touch the exact middle of each line. |
| `srt` | Touch the screen as fast as you can when the green circle appears. |
| `sh_a` | Here are some blue circles of different sizes. Touch them in order of size: the smallest first, then the next bigger one, up to the biggest. Go as quickly as you can. |
| `sh_b` | Now there are blue and orange circles. Touch them from smallest to biggest, changing colour every time: the smallest blue, then the smallest orange, then the next blue, then the next orange, and so on. Go as quickly as you can. |

The examiner-rated apathy and mood items, the 4AT and GAD-2 entries are examiner-facing and stay in English. Give the 4AT and GAD-2 from their official translations.
